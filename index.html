What’s UFRV again?

UFRV = Unit Filter Run Volume

Cars all have different size gas tanks. So how do we compare their fuel efficiency? We compare their miles per gallon (MPG).

It’s the same thing with water filters! They all come in different shapes and sizes and often run at different flow rates.

To compare them fairly, we measure total water filtered per square foot of filter media during a complete run cycle (gal/ft²).
